Who Wants to Binge a Million?

Fifteen questions about Netflix's biggest recent shows and films. Each one is worth more than the last. How far can you climb?

  • 1. Climb the ladderAnswer 15 multiple-choice questions, from $100 up to $1,000,000. They get harder as you go.
  • 2. Lock it inPick an answer, then press Final answer. There's no clock, so take your time.
  • 3. Safe havensReach $1,000 and $32,000 to lock in that money. A wrong answer drops you back to your last safe haven.
  • 4. Walk awayNot sure? Walk away before answering and keep everything you've banked so far.
  • 5. Three lifelines50:50 removes two wrong answers. Phone a colleague gives you a helpful hint. Ask the audience shows how other players voted. Each can be used once per game.
  • 6. Your progress countsYour result is recorded when you finish, and your game is saved if you need to step away.

Keyboard: A B C D to choose, Enter to lock in, 1 2 3 for lifelines.
